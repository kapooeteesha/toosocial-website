Queen Couture.

From an Amritsar store to a digitally enabled fashion brand preparing for Canada.

An established fashion retailer wanted stronger online discovery, streamlined order and inventory workflows and the infrastructure to support an international growth ambition.

THE BRIEF

More than an Amritsar storefront.

Queen Couture wanted to develop an established fashion retail business into a brand that customers could discover digitally and that the team could operate at larger scale, including preparing for expansion into Canada.

THE TASKS

Build the front end—and the systems behind it.

  • Establish Instagram, LinkedIn and Pinterest presence.
  • Develop a website for collections and product discovery.
  • Create an order management application.
  • Develop an inventory management system.
  • Support the brand's ongoing Canada expansion planning.
OUR APPROACH

Desirability on the outside.
Clarity on the inside.

We mapped the journey from discovering a collection to managing an order and keeping stock visible. The project connected brand storytelling with digital commerce and operational workflows.

CASE STUDY 01 / FMCG & MANUFACTURING

Welnis.

From a manufacturing mill to a consumer brand with national ambitions.

For a Punjab-based edible-oil and food-essentials manufacturer, the challenge was moving from production capability to a recognisable consumer-facing brand and coordinated digital presence.

Brand StrategyDigital PresenceMarket Expansion
W.

THE BRIEF

Manufacturing strength. No consistent digital presence.

Welnis had an established manufacturing base and ambitions to become a recognised national consumer brand. The priority was to build a clear identity, create digital touchpoints, strengthen trade communication and develop a market-entry approach for Kolkata and Odisha.

THE TASKS

Make the brand ready for consumers.

  • Define a consumer-facing brand direction and messaging framework.
  • Establish Instagram and an organised website experience.
  • Create offline communication for trade and brand consistency.
  • Plan regional positioning and communication for new target markets.
OUR APPROACH

From product categories to
a connected brand presence.

We assessed category communication, competitor positioning and target customers. That informed Welnis' positioning and a coordinated digital-and-offline communication direction.
